Private member program

Member-to-Member Benefits

A proposed private benefit exchange for eligible BIA businesses. The program is not active and requires BIA approval.

Proposed program framework

  • Participation would be voluntary and offered on consistent terms to eligible members.
  • Each participating business would have one standing directory listing.
  • Participating businesses would set and honour their own terms, exclusions, and expiry dates.
  • Any approved offers would be visible only through an appropriately secured member system and would not be promoted publicly by the BIA.
