Proposed program framework

  • Participation would be voluntary, using consistent eligibility requirements.
  • Each participating business would have one standing member listing.
  • Businesses would set and honour their own terms, exclusions and expiration dates.
  • Offers would not be publicly advertised or promoted by the BIA.
  • Participation would begin only after approval and a secure member-access arrangement.

Program principles

For BIA members, not the public.

The intention is a fair, consistent directory of voluntary business-to-business benefits. The BIA would provide an organized private listing space, not act as the merchant or guarantee individual offers.

No member contact details or offer terms should be entered in the public GitHub repository.
