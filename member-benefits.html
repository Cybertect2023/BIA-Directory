Private member program

Member-to-Member Benefits

Voluntary benefits shared privately between eligible BIA businesses. This is not public advertising.

How the program works

  • Participation is voluntary and available on consistent terms to eligible members.
  • Each participating business receives one standing directory listing.
  • Businesses set and honour their own terms, conditions, exclusions, and expiry dates.
  • Offers remain inside this restricted resource centre and are not publicly promoted by the BIA.
