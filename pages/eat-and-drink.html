More than somewhere to eat

Some of the best parts of a visit happen around the table.

The first sip after a country drive. The familiar faces at a village café. A good meal before the lights go down at Stirling Festival Theatre. They may seem like little moments, but they are what visitors remember.

Our local food businesses help turn an outing into an afternoon, or an afternoon into an evening. Discover their stories, explore the menus they share, and support the people serving our community.

Start exploring: six food businesses from the current Stirling-Rawdon BIA directory, all located on Mill Street or West Front Street in the village.

Food Boutique Stirling

A stop for something delicious now, and something special to bring home.

Part specialty food shop, part coffee bar, Food Boutique offers espresso drinks, fresh grab-and-go selections, prepared meals and gifts for food lovers. Hastings County highlights handcrafted lemonades, a hot table and fresh or frozen meals, too. Food Boutique shares its owners with nearby The Smokin' Barrel, making West Front Street an easy place to combine a specialty food stop with a sit-down meal.

A performance worth making an evening of

Come for the show.
Stay for the meal.

Stirling Festival Theatre brings visitors here from across the region. Your evening can begin long before the curtain rises.

Pick a performance, arrive with time to spare and choose a local dining stop along West Front Street or Mill Street. Even a quick meal or coffee makes a theatre trip part of a bigger village experience.

Local food and live performance have already worked together here. In September 2026, Farm Girl Cafe supplied high tea for the theatre’s Tea for Two & Two for Tee dinner-theatre production.
