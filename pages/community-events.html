Come be part of something

The best memories are made together.

In Stirling-Rawdon, community traditions bring neighbours and visitors into the same space. An afternoon market becomes a conversation with a grower. A concert becomes dinner downtown. A holiday gathering becomes a reason to return next year.

This guide introduces our signature events and local organizers. For a specific date, time or change of plans, always use the latest organizer announcement.

A village worth visiting in November

Holiday traditions with a hometown heart.

When autumn turns to winter, Mill Street and Farmtown Park take on a different kind of charm. Make an evening or afternoon of it, and support the small businesses and community groups that put these gatherings together.

Downtown StirlingSeasonal market

Friday, November 6, 2026 · 4–8 p.m.

Holiday Market in the Village

Mill Street hosts a festive outdoor market celebrating local vendors and the start of the holiday season. Explore downtown, discover gifts made and sold locally, and check the market organizer's announcements as the day approaches.

Mill Street · Downtown Stirling

Deck the Halls

Looking for the festive tree celebration at the park? Farmtown Park's 2026 event is Deck the Halls, featuring decorated Christmas trees, colourful wreaths, raffle prizes, refreshments and Santa photo opportunities at Heritage Village.

Farmtown Park · 437 West Front Street, Stirling

Admission: $5 · Children five and under: free. Santa visits Friday and Saturday; the organizer lists exact hours.

A year of things to look forward to

The traditions that shape our seasons.

Not every gathering has next year's dates confirmed. These recurring community favourites give you an idea of when to plan a return trip.

SpringMusic & new beginnings

Music, performances & the first outdoor gatherings

Stirling Festival of Sacred Praise and other local arts programming bring performers and audiences together, while community groups prepare for a new season of activities.

SummerVillage pride & rural roots

Canada Day, Paint the Town Red & Stirling Fair

Canada Day celebrations and the downtown Paint the Town Red shopping event bring people into the village in July. August brings the Stirling Agricultural Fair and a celebration of the region's rural heritage.

Paint the Town Red feature ↗Stirling Agricultural Society ↗
AutumnHarvest & live music

Market in the Village, harvest gatherings & StirlingFest

Local growers and makers bring seasonal favourites to the Covered Bridge parking area during the market season. StirlingFest brings classical and jazz performances to historic St. John's Anglican Church.

There's community between the festivals

The smaller gatherings matter, too.

Community dinners, library workshops, regular skating, local performances and volunteer-led activities bring people together throughout the year. These programs are often recurring, and organizers are the most reliable source for the next occurrence.
