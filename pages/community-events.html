Mark the seasons

Community traditions to look out for

Summer

Stirling Agricultural Fair

The annual fair brings the community to the Stirling fairgrounds for agricultural displays, competitions, entertainment, and family activities. Dates and program details are set by the organizers each year.

Stirling Fair official site

Growing season

Market in the Village

The seasonal Friday farmers’ market brings local growers, food producers, bakers, and makers to the Covered Bridge parking area. The 2026 season ran June 26–October 2; follow the market for the next season’s schedule and weekly vendor updates.

Market in the Village updates · Hastings County market listing

Spring

Stirling Festival of Sacred Praise

An annual festival of instrumental and vocal music, scripture, and public speaking, with participants of different ages and abilities. The organizers publish the program and schedule each year.

More than a date on the calendar

Local events are made by local people

Markets, fairs, performances, library programs, and fundraisers depend on the time and care of organizers, volunteers, vendors, and community groups. If you are visiting, come ready to support the people who make these gatherings possible.

Community dinners and benefit events are announced by their organizers. We link to active listings instead of keeping a second, easily outdated schedule here.

Before you go

Confirm the details

  • Check the organizer’s latest post or official event page.
  • Confirm location, time, admission, and registration details.
  • For seasonal markets and farm activities, check weather and availability.
