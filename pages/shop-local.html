A village of interesting finds

There's more to a shop than what's on the shelf.

Every independent storefront adds something to Stirling-Rawdon: a place to discover a new author, pick out a gift, find a finishing touch for your home, or talk with someone who knows their craft.

This guide introduces some of the distinctive shopping destinations in the BIA directory, then connects them to places to eat, things to do, and the events that bring people downtown.

An afternoon here is about more than checking errands off a list. Come curious. Leave with a story.

Books, stationery & thoughtful extras Another
chapter. 22 Mill Street · New location

Stirling Book Company

For the next great read, a gift for a book lover, or an excuse to get happily lost among the shelves.

This independent bookstore carries books across genres, children's reads, games, puzzles, cards and stationery. They also offer gift cards and special orders, and regularly connect readers through book-related events.

Recently moved: The store's current website lists 22 Mill Street, a few doors from its former 36 Mill Street address.

with care. Handcrafted in Stirling-Rawdon

Rawdon Creek Soap Co.

Small-batch, locally crafted goods with an unmistakable connection to the community.

A family-run maker of handcrafted soaps, candles, whipped tallow balms and other bath and body products. Their work blends hands-on production with local sourcing, and makes a thoughtful gift to take home from the area.

Visit planning: Online sources currently show conflicting retail addresses. Confirm the pickup or shopping location directly before travelling.

Rustic Routes

Furniture and furnishings with personality, plus unexpected finds for the home and wardrobe.

Browse a locally operated interior design and home furnishings destination offering custom furniture, upholstery, drapery, blinds and fashion. A place to gather inspiration for your next project as well as a unique everyday find.

Vintage treasures, antiques & décor Find
the one. 25 Mill Street

The Vintage Junction

A good place to look for something with a little history and a lot of personality.

Part of Mill Street's appeal is stumbling on a vintage piece or decorative find you didn't know you needed. The Vintage Junction brings together antiques, repurposed pieces and décor, making every visit a chance to discover something different.

Food Boutique Stirling

Something delicious for your day, or a treat to bring home to someone else.

A specialty food and gift store with coffee, fresh grab-and-go selections, prepared meals and other tasty discoveries. Its location beside The Smokin' Barrel makes it a natural stop when combining downtown shopping with food.

A gift that says more

Bring home a little of the village.

Whether you're shopping for a birthday, a thank-you, a host gift or the holidays, small-town shops have a way of making the search feel personal.

Try a hand-poured candle, a novel recommended by a bookseller, something for the home, a bouquet, or a gourmet treat. A good gift comes with a story about where you found it.
