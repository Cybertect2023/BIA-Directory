A village and its countryside

Small-town pace. A lot to discover.

Stirling-Rawdon brings together a walkable historic village and the farms, hamlets, and open country around it. Independent businesses sit close to community spaces; local agriculture is part of everyday life; and volunteers help keep long-running events and arts programs going.

That mix makes a visit easy to shape. Spend a morning downtown, take a short drive to a farm experience, then return for a meal or an evening performance.
