Farms & Local Food

Agriculture shapes the landscape and the life of Stirling-Rawdon. Meet local producers, look for seasonal market days, and make a direct connection with the people growing and making food here.

The village gathers around local food

Market in the Village

In season, the Stirling farmers’ market brings growers, food producers, bakers, and makers together in the Covered Bridge parking area downtown. The market is a good place to meet vendors and see what is in season; the lineup changes from week to week.

The 2026 weekly season ran from June 26 to October 2. Check the market’s own channels or the BIA calendar for the next season’s dates and vendor details before travelling.

Farm shop & tours

Ontario Water Buffalo Farm

At 3346 Stirling-Marmora Road, this working dairy farm raises a herd of water buffalo. Hastings County lists its Buff Stuff Store with buffalo milk, cheese, meat, and other farm products, as well as self-guided visits and private tours.

Animal experience

Alpaca Ridge Farms

Located at 152 Smith Road, Alpaca Ridge Farms offers alpaca experiences including barn tours and hand feeding through scheduled visits. Check availability and booking details with the farm before travelling.

Seasonal pick-your-own

Cooney’s Farms pumpkin patch

Hastings County’s farm and agriculture guide lists the Pumpkin Patch at Cooney’s Farms at 10 Wellmans Road in Stirling-Rawdon. The visit is seasonal; confirm opening dates and arrangements directly before going.

Agricultural heritage

Farmtown Park

The Hastings County Museum of Agricultural Heritage shares the story of farming in the region through its museum and heritage village. Check the museum’s current schedule and special event listings.
