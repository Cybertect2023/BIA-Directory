Market in the Village

A Friday afternoon tradition built around the best of our own backyard.

In season, local growers, food producers and makers gather at the Covered Bridge parking lot in downtown Stirling. It's a place to pick up seasonal ingredients, discover small-batch goods, and meet the people who make them.

Vendors change throughout the season, so every visit can bring something different. Check the organizer's latest posts for market dates, participating producers and special happenings.

Farm shops & countryside experiences

These independently operated farms offer a taste of local agriculture, whether you're shopping for something made here or arranging an experience with the animals.

Apples, preserves & farm goods✳

The Apple Store at Cooney Farms

10 Wellmans Road, Stirling

A family-run countryside stop with locally grown apples in season, fresh cider, preserves, cheeses and gifts. The Cooney family also sells its own farm-raised beef. Part food destination, part country store, and well worth a stop on the way through.

Working dairy farm & farm store✳

Ontario Water Buffalo & The Buff Stuff Store

3346 Stirling-Marmora Road, Stirling

Meet an unusual herd in the Hastings County countryside and discover farm-made products at The Buff Stuff Store. Shop for buffalo dairy and meat products, enjoy gelato, or find out about farm visits. Self-guided visitors are asked to check in at the store first.

Alpaca encounters & farm-made goods✳

Alpaca Ridge Farms

152 Smith Road, Stirling

Get to know the alpacas on a guided barn visit, meet the herd and explore products made from alpaca fibre. The Barry family's farm offers book-ahead experiences and seasonal activities. It is not a drop-in attraction, so reserve before setting out.

Our farming roots

Agriculture is part of the story here.

Farming in Stirling-Rawdon isn't simply a backdrop. It's part of the community's history, economy and everyday life, from country markets to generations of family-run operations.

Farmtown Park, home of the Hastings County Museum of Agricultural Heritage, brings that story to life through rural heritage exhibits and a glimpse of the tools and traditions that helped shape the region.
