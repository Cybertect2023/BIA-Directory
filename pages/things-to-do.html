Things to Do in Stirling-Rawdon

Live performance, agricultural heritage, village shopping, and outdoor stops all fit into an easy visit. Start with a favourite, then give yourself time to explore.

Culture and heritage

A village with stories to see and hear

Live performance

Stirling Festival Theatre

Housed in a historic building dating to 1927, the theatre presents live entertainment throughout the year, from stage productions and concerts to comedy and its annual pantomime. Check the official schedule for current shows and tickets.

See the theatre’s current program

Agricultural heritage

Farmtown Park

Visit the Hastings County Museum of Agricultural Heritage and Heritage Village to explore the region’s farming history. The fairgrounds are also home to community events; check opening times before arriving.

Township visitor information

437 West Front Street, Stirling

Local knowledge

Stirling-Rawdon Public Library

The library offers free programs for adults and children, exhibitions, and community activities. Browse its current program listings to find what is on during your visit.

Village wandering

Independent shops and places to eat

Walk Mill Street and the surrounding downtown blocks. Find a gift, stop for a coffee or lunch, and support the small businesses that keep the village lively.

Fresh air close to town

Parks, paths, and the covered bridge

Choose a local park or playground, follow the Heritage Trail near Station Park, or explore a section of the Trans Canada Trail. The Township’s parks page lists locations such as Station Park, Henry Street Park, Springbrook Ball Diamond and Park, and Kings Mill Conservation Area.

Trail surfaces and conditions vary. Check current municipal information and use care around water, roads, and seasonal conditions.

A simple itinerary

One unhurried day

Morning

Coffee, breakfast, and a browse

Start at a local café, then explore the independent businesses downtown. Check opening hours before your trip.

Midday

Choose a local experience

Visit Farmtown Park, plan a farm tour, or spend time outdoors on a local path or at a park.

Afternoon

Make a little more time for town

Stop by the library or continue exploring the village shops and food businesses.

Evening

Dinner and a show

Book a performance at Stirling Festival Theatre and enjoy a meal in town before or after the show.
