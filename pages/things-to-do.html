A little different, in the best way

Come for an experience.
Stay for the village.

Stirling-Rawdon offers an easy mix of arts, agricultural history, countryside scenery and people who take pride in their businesses. From a theatre seat to a quiet picnic stop, you can shape a visit around the pace that suits you.

Start with these local favourites, then leave time for the unexpected discoveries.

Arts, performances & memories

Two reasons to make the trip

Big experiences don't always require a big-city address. Discover a stage with character and a museum devoted to the region's rural roots.

Live entertainment✳

Stirling Festival Theatre

Inside a historic 1927 building on West Front Street, the theatre brings live plays, comedy, concerts and seasonal performances to the village. Choose a show, then add dinner downtown to make an evening of it.

41 West Front Street, Stirling

Rural heritage✳

Farmtown Park

Step into rural Ontario's past at the Hastings County Museum of Agricultural Heritage. Explore a recreated heritage village, farming equipment, stories of butter and cheese making, and exhibits for curious visitors of all ages.

437 West Front Street, Stirling

Plan a museum visit ↗ Seasonal: May long weekend to the end of September

Slow down, step outside

Fresh air is never far away.

One of the pleasures of Stirling-Rawdon is how quickly the village gives way to a creek, a leafy path or a quiet stretch of countryside.

Henry Street Park & the covered bridge

A family-friendly park at 85 Henry Street sits beside Rawdon Creek. The nearby covered bridge and village streets make an easy addition to a relaxed walk through town.

Station Park & the Heritage Trail

Follow the old railway corridor from the village. Station Park sits near the former train station, with a trail connection for a peaceful walk or cycle.

King's Mill Conservation Area

On Wellmans Road, this quiet conservation property has views of Squires Creek and a historic grist mill. The mill is closed to visitors; the grounds are better suited to photography, nature appreciation and a picnic than a marked hike.

Make it your kind of outing

More ways to enjoy Stirling

Families, sports fans and the curious will find plenty of smaller stops that make a visit memorable.

Programs, art & activities

Stirling-Rawdon Public Library

Explore rotating local art exhibitions and browse the library's schedule of workshops, family activities and special programs. A welcoming indoor stop in the centre of the village.

43 West Front Street

Year-round indoor golf

The Fairway Golf

Play virtual courses or practise your swing on Trackman simulators. Sessions are booked online, making it an option for golf lovers when the weather doesn't cooperate.

400 West Front Street, Building 1, Unit 3

Don't rush through downtown

A good day includes a few local stops.

Stirling's independent shops and cafés are part of the attraction. Browse new books at Stirling Book Company, discover handcrafted goods at Rawdon Creek Soap Co., or pause for lunch at one of the village's locally operated restaurants.

The BIA directory can help you find businesses, addresses and links before you visit.
