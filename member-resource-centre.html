03 · Private member program

Member-to-Member Benefits

Voluntary benefits offered privately between eligible BIA businesses. This is not public advertising and the BIA does not negotiate or guarantee transactions.

How the program works

  • Participation is voluntary and available on consistent terms to eligible members.
  • Each participating business receives one standing directory listing.
  • Businesses set and honour their own terms, conditions, exclusions, and expiry dates.
  • Offers stay within this restricted resource centre and are not publicly promoted by the BIA.

The private benefits directory will be added after eligibility and program guidelines are finalized.
