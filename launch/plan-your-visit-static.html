A little planning goes a long way

Before you head out

Parking & getting around

Use the theatre’s published parking guidance for a downtown visit. It asks visitors to avoid Emma Street and the private BMO, GayLea and Stirling Towers lots. Check signs when you arrive.

Accessible parking-bay locations have not yet been verified for this guide. Contact your destination for suitable parking and entrance arrangements.

Theatre parking information ↗

Washrooms & access

The Township identifies washrooms in the brick building near Henry Street Park’s ball field. Opening dates and hours need confirmation.

The theatre reports washrooms on its main-stage lobby level and wheelchair seating by advance arrangement. Contact the box office at least 24 hours ahead to reserve that seating.
